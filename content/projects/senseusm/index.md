A USM Bridging research grant to investigate the feasibility of setting up a smart environmental sensing with low-cost IoT sensors.

<!--more-->

This project explores the feasibility and accuracy of using low-cost IoT sensors for the purpose of a smart environmental sensing platform within USM.

Project Duration: **25/01/2018-24/07/2019**

Grant No: **304/PNAV/6316071** (Bridging Grant - Mentor)

Funding: RM 9,990.00
