An APNIC ISIF Asia grant project to support emerging National Research and Education Networks (NRENs) in Southeast Asia in establishing Security Operations Centre (SOC) capabilities using open-source technologies.

<!--more-->

Implemented through BitRanger and supported through the ISIF Asia Grants programme of the APNIC Foundation, the initiative covers participating communities in Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, and Timor-Leste. It focuses on building a collaborative framework that enables participating networks to monitor threats, share threat intelligence, and strengthen cybersecurity resilience across regional research and education infrastructures, including:

- Building practical SOC operational capabilities
- Training NREN cybersecurity personnel
- Deploying pilot Security Operations Centres
- Establishing collaborative threat intelligence capabilities and sharing Indicators of Compromise
- Integrating honeypot and network telescope observations
- Developing SOC deployment toolkits and playbooks
- Strengthening cybersecurity policies associated with SOC operations

Consultancy Project (Principal Consultant).
