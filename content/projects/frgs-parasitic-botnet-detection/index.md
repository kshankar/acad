An FRGS research grant to study and propose methods to detect parasitic botnets in DHT-based P2P networks.

<!--more-->

National Grant (Project Leader). Parasitic botnets coexist with benign nodes and services in a Distributed Hash Table (DHT)-based Peer-to-Peer (P2P) network; recent years have observed the emergence of new parasitic botnets on the BitTorrent and IPFS networks. This project proposed a more effective method to detect such botnets within DHT-based P2P networks.
