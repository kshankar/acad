An FRGS research grant to design mechanisms to secure SECS/GEM communications in manufacturing industry environments.

<!--more-->

Industrial equipment increasingly relies on the SECS/GEM protocol for machine-to-machine communication, yet the protocol was not designed with security in mind. This project studies and proposes mechanisms to secure SECS/GEM communications for Industry 4.0 manufacturing environments, complementing related work on [ES-SECS/GEM](/publications/2023-laghari-es-secs-gem-an-efficient-security-mechan/) and [SECS/GEMsec](/publications/2021-laghari-secs-gemsec-a-mechanism-for-detection-an/).

Project Duration: **07/09/2021-06/09/2023**

Funding: RM 76,700.00, Fundamental Research Grant Scheme (FRGS)
