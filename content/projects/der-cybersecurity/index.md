An international research collaboration studying cybersecurity challenges for distributed energy resources (DER) and IoT controller deployment models in Malaysia.

<!--more-->

This project enhances cybersecurity for distributed energy resources in Malaysia, and includes a related study of IoT controller deployment models within the distributed energy resources environment (RM 64,092.00, 01/01/2023-31/03/2024) — building on earlier work on [Cyber-Physical Security Design for Energy Aggregation](/projects/cyber-physical-energy-aggregation/).

Project Duration: **01/08/2023-31/01/2026**

Funding: RM 316,134.38 (External Agency, International)
