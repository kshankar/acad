An international research collaboration under the ERIA CPSF-ERAB initiative on cyber and physical system security in the energy domain.

<!--more-->

This project investigates cyber and physical system security challenges relevant to Malaysia's energy infrastructure, conducted under the Economic Research Institute for ASEAN and East Asia (ERIA) Cyber and Physical System Security Framework - ERIA Energy Research and Analysis Bureau (CPSF-ERAB) initiative.

Project Duration: **30/12/2025-30/12/2026**

Funding: RM 259,240.00 (External Agency, International)
